## Why protein matters on a GLP-1 medicine

GLP-1 medicines like Ozempic, Wegovy, Zepbound, and Mounjaro help many people eat less and lose weight. But when you lose weight, you don’t only lose fat. You can also lose muscle.

In one large study of a GLP-1 medicine, about 38% of the weight people lost was lean mass, which includes muscle. A 2025 advisory from four US nutrition and obesity groups lists keeping muscle and bone as a key goal during treatment.

Muscle helps you stay strong, move well, and keep your energy up. Two things help protect it: **enough protein** and **strength exercise**.

## How much protein do you need?

The 2025 advisory lists **1.2 to 1.6 grams of protein per kilogram of body weight a day** during active weight loss. That is higher than the basic adult minimum of 0.8 g/kg.

The advisory also says that for people with obesity, using your full body weight can overestimate how much protein you need. Our [protein calculator](/protein-calculator?goal=glp1) handles this for you. Pick “On a GLP-1 medication” as your goal.

## Eat protein first

When you feel full fast, the first few bites matter most. The advisory suggests eating protein-rich foods first in a meal, so you get them in before you feel full.

Try this at each meal:

1. Start with the protein: the chicken, eggs, fish, tofu, or yogurt.
2. Then eat the vegetables.
3. Finish with rice, bread, or other starches if you still have room.

## Protein-rich foods that don’t take much room

These foods give you a lot of protein in a small portion:

| Food | Portion | Protein |
|---|---|---|
| Chicken breast, roasted | 4 oz (113 g) | 35 g |
| Cottage cheese | 1 cup (226 g) | 24 g |
| Plain Greek yogurt, nonfat | 1 cup (226 g) | 23 g |
| Canned tuna in water, drained | 4 oz (113 g) | 22 g |
| Firm tofu | 4 oz (113 g) | 20 g |
| Eggs | 3 large | 19 g |
| Lentils, cooked | 1 cup (198 g) | 18 g |

Values come from USDA FoodData Central.

The advisory encourages protein from beans, peas, lentils, whole grains, dairy, seafood, eggs, and lean poultry.

## Smaller meals, more often

If big meals feel like too much, try four or five smaller ones. Give each one a protein food. A cup of Greek yogurt at 3 p.m. counts just as much as the protein at dinner.

## Add strength exercise

Protein works best with strength exercise, like lifting weights, resistance bands, or bodyweight moves. The advisory lists resistance training as a key way to keep muscle. Ask your care team what is safe for you before you start.

## The bottom line

On a GLP-1 medicine, aim for enough protein, eat it first, and add strength exercise when you can. Small, protein-rich meals and snacks make it easier.
