This guide makes that choice easier. It covers McDonald's calories in India for the most popular burgers, nuggets, wraps, and fries, plus how much protein each one gives you. If you want a fuller meal without guessing, you're in the right place.

## Where these numbers come from

Let me explain the sources, because it matters here.

The official numbers come from McDonald's India's North & East nutrition booklet (Connaught Plaza Restaurants, December 2025). The menu in West and South India may differ a little. The booklet rounds its values and does not list fiber.

A few popular items, like the McSpicy Chicken Burger and the Chicken Maharaja Mac, were not in that booklet. For those, we used FatSecret India. FatSecret is a third-party nutrition site, not McDonald's India. Treat those numbers as a good guide, not the final word.

## The best high-protein picks at McDonald's India

When I sorted our McDonald's India data by protein, a few items stood out. I also worked out protein per 100 calories for each. This is my own simple math from the listed numbers, and it shows how much protein you get for the calories you spend.

### Cheesy Chicken Burger: the official protein leader

The [Cheesy Chicken Burger](/chains/mcdonalds-india/cheesy-chicken-burger) has 29.9 g of protein and 497 calories in the official booklet. That works out to about 6 g of protein per 100 calories, the best ratio of any burger in our data.

One thing to know: it also has 1,376 mg of sodium. If you watch your salt for blood pressure or kidney reasons, talk to your doctor or a registered dietitian about how it fits.

### Chicken Maharaja Mac calories

FatSecret India lists the [Chicken Maharaja Mac](/chains/mcdonalds-india/chicken-maharaja-mac) at 689 calories and 34 g of protein. That is the most protein of any burger we track here, at about 4.9 g per 100 calories.

It is a big burger, though. FatSecret lists 36.69 g of fat, so it works best as a full meal on its own, not with fries on the side.

### Chicken nuggets calories, piece by piece

The official booklet lists [Chicken Nuggets (6 pc)](/chains/mcdonalds-india/chicken-nuggets-6-pc) at 253 calories and 15 g of protein. That's about 5.9 g of protein per 100 calories, which is close to the Cheesy Chicken Burger.

If you're wondering about one nugget, here's the math. Dividing the official 6-piece numbers by 6 gives about 42 calories and 2.5 g of protein per nugget. This is calculated, and real nugget sizes vary.

FatSecret India also lists other pack sizes:

- [Chicken Nuggets (4 pc)](/chains/mcdonalds-india/chicken-nuggets-4-pc): 169 calories, 10.03 g protein
- [Chicken Nuggets (9 pc)](/chains/mcdonalds-india/chicken-nuggets-9-pc): 378 calories, 22.56 g protein
- [Chicken Nuggets (20 pc)](/chains/mcdonalds-india/chicken-nuggets-20-pc): 812 calories, 47.6 g protein

### Chicken McGrill Burger: the lighter pick

The [Chicken McGrill Burger](/chains/mcdonalds-india/chicken-mcgrill-burger) has 274 calories, 13.2 g of protein, and 8.5 g of fat. It's the lowest-calorie chicken burger in the official booklet. The [Butter Chicken Grilled Burger](/chains/mcdonalds-india/butter-chicken-grilled-burger) is another grilled option at 357 calories and 17.1 g of protein.

## McSpicy Chicken Burger calories and other favorites

Here's where it gets interesting. Some of the most loved items are not the best protein deals.

FatSecret India lists the [McSpicy Chicken Burger](/chains/mcdonalds-india/mcspicy-chicken-burger) at 450 calories and 21.46 g of protein. That's a solid middle pick. The [McSpicy Chicken Wrap](/chains/mcdonalds-india/mcspicy-chicken-wrap) comes in at 567 calories and 23.74 g of protein, also per FatSecret India.

McAloo Tikki calories are lower than many people expect. The official booklet lists the [Aloo Tikki Burger](/chains/mcdonalds-india/aloo-tikki-burger) at 340 calories. But it has just 8.5 g of protein, which is about 2.5 g per 100 calories. It's a light snack, not a protein meal.

## Best vegetarian picks for protein

Vegetarian options at McDonald's India tend to have more calories for the same protein. Still, you have good choices:

- [McSpicy Paneer Wrap](/chains/mcdonalds-india/mcspicy-paneer-wrap): 675 calories, 21 g protein
- [McSpicy Paneer Burger](/chains/mcdonalds-india/mcspicy-paneer-burger): 647 calories, 20.3 g protein
- [Veg Maharaja Mac](/chains/mcdonalds-india/veg-maharaja-mac): 833 calories, 24.2 g protein
- [McVeggie Burger](/chains/mcdonalds-india/mcveggie-burger): 388 calories, 10.2 g protein

If you eat eggs, the [Egg McMuffin](/chains/mcdonalds-india/egg-mcmuffin) is worth a look for breakfast. It has 283 calories and 14.1 g of protein, which is about 5 g per 100 calories.

## Sides and desserts add up fast

A burger is only part of the order. The official booklet lists [Fries (Medium)](/chains/mcdonalds-india/fries-medium) at 305 calories and only 4.8 g of protein.

FatSecret India lists [Fries (Large)](/chains/mcdonalds-india/fries-large) at 431 calories and [Fries (Regular)](/chains/mcdonalds-india/fries-regular) at 216 calories. A [Soft Serve Cone](/chains/mcdonalds-india/soft-serve-cone) is 86 calories on FatSecret India, which makes it one of the smallest treats on the menu.

Here's a simple example. A McSpicy Chicken Burger (450 calories, FatSecret India) plus medium fries (305 calories, official) comes to 755 calories. The fries add a lot of calories but very little protein.

## McDonald's high protein meal ideas

This part is actually really easy. Pick one protein-rich main, then add a second protein item instead of fries if you're still hungry. All numbers below come from the official booklet.

1. **Big protein meal:** Cheesy Chicken Burger + 6 pc nuggets = 750 calories and 44.9 g protein.
2. **Lighter protein meal:** Chicken McGrill Burger + 6 pc nuggets = 527 calories and 28.2 g protein.
3. **Breakfast:** Egg McMuffin = 283 calories and 14.1 g protein. Or try the [Sausage McMuffin](/chains/mcdonalds-india/sausage-mcmuffin) at 281 calories and 16.3 g protein.

How much protein do you need in a day? The Dietary Guidelines for Americans 2025 to 2030 set a goal of 1.2 to 1.6 g of protein per kilogram of body weight a day. For a 154 lb (70 kg) adult, that's 84 to 112 g. Our [protein calculator](/protein-calculator) can give you your own range, and our guide on [how much protein per meal](/guides/how-much-protein-per-meal) shows how to spread it out.

If you have kidney disease, diabetes, or are pregnant, talk to your doctor or a registered dietitian before changing how much protein you eat.

## What it takes to walk off a McDonald's meal

I like this as a reality check, not as a rule that you have to "earn" your food.

The 2024 Adult Compendium of Physical Activities gives brisk walking (3.5 to 3.9 mph, or about 5.6 to 6.3 km/h) a value of 4.8 METs. A MET is a way to measure how hard an activity is compared with sitting still. The formula is:

**Calories burned ≈ MET × body weight (kg) × hours**

For a 154 lb (70 kg) person, that's 4.8 × 70 × 1 = about 336 calories per hour, or about 5.6 calories a minute. So:

- **Aloo Tikki Burger (340 calories):** about 61 minutes of brisk walking
- **Fries (Medium) (305 calories):** about 54 minutes
- **Veg Maharaja Mac (833 calories):** about 149 minutes, or roughly 2.5 hours

You can plug in your own weight and activity with our [calories burned calculator](/calories-burned-calculator).

## My take

Based on what I've seen in the data, the smartest McDonald's orders in India are simple. Lead with a chicken item, skip or shrink the fries, and add nuggets if you need more protein.

For vegetarians, the paneer items give the most protein, but they cost more calories. That's fine if it fits your day. It just helps to know it before you order.

I'd also check the official page now and then. Menus change, so the numbers can change too.

## The bottom line

McDonald's calories in India range from light snacks like the 86-calorie soft serve cone to big meals like the 833-calorie Veg Maharaja Mac. For the most protein per calorie, the Cheesy Chicken Burger and 6-piece nuggets lead the official list. The Chicken McGrill is a great lighter pick.

You don't have to give up McDonald's to eat well. A little planning at the counter goes a long way, and now you have the numbers to do it.
