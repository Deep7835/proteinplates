## Why protein matters on a GLP-1

GLP-1 medicines help many people eat less. But when you lose weight, some of it can be muscle, not just fat. A 2025 advisory from four US nutrition and obesity groups lists keeping muscle as a key goal during treatment. Its two main tools are enough protein and strength exercise.

Eating less also means every bite counts more. A clear protein target helps you make the most of the food you do eat.

## What this version sets up for you

This page starts with the goal set to "On a GLP-1 medication." That changes two things:

- **Protein:** 1.2 to 1.6 grams per kilogram of body weight a day, the range from the 2025 advisory. If your BMI is over 30, we use the weight at a BMI of 30 for your height, as the advisory warns full weight can overstate needs.
- **Calories:** a guide number 15% below maintenance. On a GLP-1, your appetite may set your intake more than any calculator. Focus on the protein number first.

For example, someone who is 5 ft 5 in (165 cm) and weighs 230 lb (104 kg) gets a target based on about 180 lb (82 kg). That works out to about 100 to 130 grams a day, or about 33 to 43 grams at each of three meals.

## Food tips when you feel full fast

- **Protein first.** Eat the chicken, fish, eggs, or tofu before the rest of the plate.
- **Go smaller and more often.** Choose "3 meals + snack" to see a smaller per-meal target.
