## What the female setting changes

This version starts with sex set to female and the goal set to "Lose fat." Sex matters here in two ways:

- **Calories at rest.** Our calorie formula, Mifflin-St Jeor, gives women about 166 fewer calories a day than a man of the same height, weight, and age.
- **The calorie floor.** We never suggest fewer than 1,200 calories a day for women.

Protein is not changed by sex. It is set by your weight, age, and goal.

## Why protein takes a bigger share

Many women have smaller calorie budgets than men. Protein is set in grams, not as a percent. So in a smaller budget, protein fills a bigger slice.

For example, a lightly active 40-year-old woman who is 5 ft 5 in (165 cm) and 145 lb (66 kg) gets about 1,460 calories a day on the weight loss goal. Her 125 grams of protein make up about a third of those calories. That leaves carbs at about 36% of calories, below the usual 45% to 65% range. That is common on a high-protein plan and fine for most healthy adults.

## Tips for women

- **Pick the eating style you enjoy.** Lower fat, balanced, and higher fat all stay in healthy fat ranges.
- **Watch your fiber.** Smaller calorie budgets can squeeze out fiber. Our [fiber calculator](/fiber-calculator) shows your target.
- **Use the per-meal numbers.** Aim for a protein food at each meal and let carbs and fat follow.
- **Over 50?** Try the "Healthy aging (50+)" goal to compare.
