## How your weight loss macros are built

This version starts with the goal set to "Lose fat." Your plan is built in this order:

1. **Calories:** 20% below your maintenance calories, with a floor of 1,200 for women and 1,500 for men.
2. **Protein:** the middle of a 1.6 to 2.2 g/kg range. That is higher than for maintaining, because protein helps you hold on to muscle while you eat less.
3. **Fat:** 20%, 30%, or 35% of your calories, based on the eating style you pick.
4. **Carbs:** whatever calories are left.

Here is an example for a lightly active 40-year-old woman, 5 ft 5 in (165 cm) and 145 lb (66 kg), with the balanced style. She gets about 1,460 calories a day: 125 g protein, 131 g carbs, and 49 g fat. Over three meals, that is about 42 g protein, 44 g carbs, and 16 g fat per meal.

## Picking a fat level for weight loss

All three styles can work for weight loss when calories are the same. Choose the one you can stick with:

- **Lower fat** leaves more room for fruit, rice, oats, and bread.
- **Higher fat** leaves more room for eggs, cheese, nuts, and olive oil.

## Check in and adjust

Your calorie target is an estimate, not an exact number. Research also shows weight loss slows over time as your body adapts. Weigh yourself a few times a week, at the same time of day. Look at the trend over 2 to 3 weeks before you change anything.
