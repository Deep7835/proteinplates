## How this waist to height ratio calculator works

The math is simple. We divide your waist size by your height, using the same units for both.

**Waist to height ratio = waist ÷ height**

For example, if you are 5 ft 6 in (66 in, or 168 cm) tall and your waist is 32 in (81 cm), your ratio is 32 ÷ 66 = 0.48.

The calculator also shows the waist size that equals half your height. For someone who is 5 ft 6 in (168 cm), that is 33 in (84 cm). Keep your waist below that number to stay under 0.5.

## What your result means

We use the bands from the 2025 NICE guideline on overweight and obesity:

| Ratio | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Under 0.5 | Healthy. Your waist is less than half your height. |
| 0.5 to 0.59 | Increased central fat, with increased health risks |
| 0.6 or more | High central fat, with further increased health risks |

Fat around your middle is linked to health problems such as type 2 diabetes and heart disease. That is why waist size can tell you something your weight alone cannot.

NICE says the ratio can be used for adults with a BMI under 35. It works for both sexes and all ethnic groups, and for people with a lot of muscle, whose BMI can look high even when they are lean.

## How to measure your waist

1. Stand up straight with your feet together.
2. Breathe out normally. Do not suck in.
3. Find the spot halfway between your lowest rib and the top of your hip bone.
4. Wrap a tape measure around that spot. Keep it level and snug, but not tight.

Measure at the same time of day each time, like first thing in the morning.
