## Why a woman's deficit needs more care

The same deficit takes a bigger bite out of a smaller calorie budget. Many women burn fewer calories a day than men, partly because of body size. The formula we use also gives women about 166 fewer calories at rest than a man of the same height, weight, and age.

So a plan that feels easy for one person can push another close to our 1,200 calorie floor. If you hit that floor, the answer is more time, not fewer calories.

## What this version sets up for you

This page starts with female settings and a sample woman: 40 years old, 5 ft 5 in (165 cm), 145 lb (66 kg), and lightly active. Her goal is 130 lb (59 kg) in 16 weeks.

That is 15 lb (6.8 kg) to lose. The calculator shows a deficit of about 470 calories a day, so she would eat about 1,360 calories a day. That is about 0.9 lb (0.4 kg) a week, a pace inside CDC guidance.

If you ask for a faster pace than 2 lb (0.9 kg) a week, you will see a warning.

## Tips for women in a deficit

- **Keep protein high.** It helps you hold on to muscle while you eat less. Our [protein calculator for weight loss](/protein-calculator/for-weight-loss) gives you a daily target.
- **Give it more weeks.** A longer timeline means more food each day and a plan you can stick with.
- **Look at the trend.** Your weight can swing from day to day. Compare weekly averages instead.
